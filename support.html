Support

Found a bug, spotted a math mistake, or have an idea? We'd love to hear from you.

Get in touch

Email bugjugapp@gmail.com. It helps to tell us your device, the chapter or place in the story, and what you expected to happen. If you use GitHub, you can also open an issue.

Common questions

What ages is it for?

Kids from kindergarten to 5th grade, roughly ages 5 to 11. The story is written for about ages 6 to 9 and every line is spoken, so early readers can play on their own. The math adapts to the player, so older or younger kids get problems at their own level.

How do I get to the parent area?

On the title screen, tap the gear (⚙️) in the bottom corner. (From a scene, open the menu ☰ and tap Title screen first.) A grown-ups check asks you to press and hold a button for 3 seconds, then answer a quick question.

How do I change the grade?

In the parent area, open the Settings tab and pick a grade from K to 5. You can also choose whether to follow the NWEA MAP Growth skill plan for that grade, or only grade-level topics. The game adjusts from the next problem on.

Does it help with the MAP Growth test?

It practices the skills MAP Growth measures. With MAP Growth turned on, problems follow the skills on your child's grade's MAP Growth plan, which reaches well below and above grade level (grades 4 and 5 include grade 6 topics). You can enter your child's latest scores in the parent area as starting levels, and it shows the game's estimate of their level on the RIT scale. The problems are our own, not real test questions, and the estimate isn't an official score.

How do I move progress to a new phone or tablet?

In the parent area's Settings tab, tap Export save file and send it to the new device (AirDrop, Files or email work well). On the new device, install Maple Leaf Math, open the parent area and tap Import save file. Each device keeps its own save until you do this.

I bought the full story. How do I get it on another device?

When the free chapters end, Penny asks for a grown-up. Tap I'm a grown-up, pass the check and tap Restore purchase. Signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account (or in the same Family Sharing group on Apple devices), the unlock comes back at no charge.

The purchase says it's waiting for approval.

That's Ask to Buy: the family organizer gets a request on their device. Once they approve it, the story unlocks.

My child is stuck on a puzzle.

Wrong answers bring hints, then a picture, then a step-by-step walkthrough and a fresh problem, so no one stays stuck for long. The Teach me how button on every question opens a short lesson with Ms. Juniper. You can always walk or bike back to places you've already visited, too.

Does it need the internet?

No. Maple Leaf Math works fully offline, even in airplane mode.

Is the free part really free?

Yes. The prologue, the first two chapters and the treehouse are free with no time limit. One $4.99 purchase unlocks the rest of the story and the bonus episodes. There are no ads, no subscriptions and nothing else for sale.
