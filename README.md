# mapleleafmath.github.io

The website for Maple Leaf Math: the home page, privacy policy and support page used by the App Store,
Google Play and Steam listings. Plain static HTML served by GitHub Pages at
https://mapleleafmath.github.io/; no build step.

- `index.html`: marketing page
- `privacy.html`: privacy policy (the stores' "Privacy Policy URL")
- `support.html`: support, FAQ, terms and credits (App Store "Support URL")
- `img/`: screenshots, icon and share image, made by `tool/store_art.sh` in the app repo (`store/web/`)
- `video/`: the trailer and its poster, made by `tool/make_trailer.sh` in the app repo (`store/web/video/`)
- `fonts/`: Fredoka (SIL Open Font License), self-hosted so the site loads nothing from third parties
